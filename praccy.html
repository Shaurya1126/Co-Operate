About Co-operator

A real-time co-op readiness platform built to help Canadian university students find, understand, and land the right co-op positions.

What is Co-operator?

Co-operator is a data-driven co-op readiness analyzer that scrapes live job postings from across Canada every day, extracts the skills employers are actually asking for, and matches them against your own skill profile. Instead of browsing job boards blindly, you get a personalized picture of exactly where you stand — and what to learn next.

Whether you're a first-year student trying to understand the market or a third-year preparing your co-op applications, Co-operator gives you the data to make smarter decisions about your career.

How It Works
Live Scraping

Co-operator pulls real co-op and internship postings from across Canada daily, keeping the data fresh and relevant.

Skill Extraction

Each job description is analyzed to extract technical and soft skills, building a real picture of what employers want right now.

Smart Matching

Upload your resume or enter your skills and get a personalized fit score against every job in the database.

Trend Tracking

Co-operator tracks which skills are rising and falling in demand over time so you always know what to learn next.

PDF Reports

Generate a full personalized co-op readiness report with job matches, skill gaps, trends, and learning resources — all in one PDF.

Degree-Aware

Results are tailored to your degree and year of study, accounting for baseline skills your program already teaches you.

What You Can Do
— Browse live co-op job postings across Canada
— Filter jobs by role, company, or specific skill
— See which technical and soft skills are most in-demand this season
— Track which skills are rising and falling in demand over the past 30 days
— Get a personalized fit score against every job in the database

Data & Accuracy

Co-operator pulls job data from public job board APIs and processes it automatically. While we do our best to extract skills and categorize roles accurately, some results may be incomplete or miscategorized depending on how employers write their job descriptions.

Fit scores are a guide, not a guarantee. Use them to identify gaps and prioritize learning — not as a definitive measure of your qualifications. Always read the full job description before applying.

Built for Canadian Co-op Students

Co-operator was built to give students the same data advantage that recruiters already have. Know what's in demand, know where you stand, and apply with confidence.
